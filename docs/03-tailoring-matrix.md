# Standards compliance and tailoring matrix

This prototype is designed to NASA standards with documented tailoring. It is **not** certified compliant with any of them. Each row says how we meet a requirement area, or why we do not.

Status key: **C** complies · **P** partially complies · **T** tailored (met a different way, with rationale) · **N/A** not applicable to a ground prototype.

## Documented deviations

These depart from a NASA convention on purpose. Each needs mentor sign-off before FDR.

| ID | Convention | Deviation | Rationale | Mitigation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| DEV-1 | NASA-STD-3001 App. F F.6.4: advisory alerts use blue | Crew prompts are red or neutral, never blue, outside alert windows | Blue light shifts the body clock and suppresses melatonin (OCHMO-TB-026); the tool exists to protect sleep | Prompts are advisory notices, not part of vehicle caution and warning; they never sound or flash; every prompt has a text label |
| DEV-2 | App. F F.6.4: red means emergency or warning | ACK key backlight and night display are red | Red has almost no circadian effect | Dim (≤ 30 % duty), steady or slow pulse; the key is labelled ACK; never used during critical blocks |
